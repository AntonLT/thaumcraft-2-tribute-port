# Alt's Thaumcraft 2 Tribute Port patches

My own patches for the [Thaumcraft 2 Tribute Port](https://modrinth.com/mod/thaumcraft-2-tribute-port). They make Thaumcraft 2 fit modern Minecraft better, by my own taste. They are **not** the gameplay Azanor intended, and nobody has endorsed them. The Tribute Port itself works without them.

## What they change

- **Vis and research values** for items that Minecraft added after 1.2.5, so crucibles and the Quaesitum treat them sensibly.
- **32 new research entries.** Basic arcane materials, machines and wands are locked behind research, each with a Thaumonomicon page.
- **Crucible vis knowledge.** Dissolving an item teaches you its vis value, and its tooltip shows the value from then on.
- **Deepslate Cinnabar Ore** and raw cinnabar.
- **A simpler Thaumonomicon recipe**, and elemental tools made in the Thaumic Infuser.
- **Curios slots on NeoForge** for charms, goggles and arcane boots, when Curios is installed.

The complete list of values is in [VALUES.md](https://github.com/AntonLT/thaumcraft-2-tribute-port/blob/main/examples/alts-tc2tp-patches/VALUES.md).

## Install

Put the jar for your loader in the `mods` folder next to the Thaumcraft 2 Tribute Port, on the client and on the server. Most of the changes also work as a datapack; see the [README](https://github.com/AntonLT/thaumcraft-2-tribute-port/blob/main/examples/alts-tc2tp-patches/README.md).
